Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Who we are

AutoTube Studio operates its own YouTube channels and an internal tool that publishes videos to them. The tool is used only by the studio's owner.

What data the app accesses

Through YouTube API Services the app accesses only YouTube channels owned by AutoTube Studio, using the scopes youtube.upload (to upload our own videos) and youtube.readonly (to read view, like and subscriber counts of our own channels and videos). The app does not request, collect, store or process data of any other YouTube user.

How data is used and stored

OAuth tokens and statistics of our own channels are stored only on our private server, are used solely to publish and measure our own videos, and are never sold, shared with or disclosed to third parties.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to the app can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept while the channels are active; tokens are deleted when access is revoked. Data deletion requests: andevush@gmail.com. We respond within 7 days.

Changes

Updates to this policy are published on this page with a new date.
